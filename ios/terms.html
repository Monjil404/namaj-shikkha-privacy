4. Prayer times, adhan and reminders

  • Prayer times are derived by astronomical calculation, based on your location, the calculation method you choose and precaution (ihtiyat) minutes. They can differ by a few minutes between localities and methods. If in doubt, give precedence to your local mosque or your country's official timetable (in Bangladesh, the Islamic Foundation Bangladesh timetable).
  • For fasting, Eid and moon sighting, the final decision rests with the moon-sighting authority of your country (in Bangladesh, the National Moon Sighting Committee).
  • Reminders are delivered by iOS. iOS plays at most about 30 seconds of a notification sound, so a full adhan is played only inside the App; Silent mode, Focus, Low Power Mode or turned-off notifications can silence or delay reminders. Please do not rely on the App as your only way to know that a prayer time has started.
