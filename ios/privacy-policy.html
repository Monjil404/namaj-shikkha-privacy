This policy covers the app as distributed on the Apple App Store, for iPhone and iPad. The app is offline-first and ad-free. It does not ask for your name, email or phone number, has no account and no login, contains no advertising SDK, does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites — which is why it never shows Apple's “Allow tracking?” prompt. Your worship records, notes, bookmarks and settings are never sent to any server — they stay on your device. What does leave the device: anonymous usage statistics and a notification token handled by Google Firebase (section 8a), an approximate location for the optional weather display (section 7), your location while the optional “Nearby mosques” page is open — sent through our server to Google Places to find mosques around you (section 6a), and a periodic check of our settings endpoint (section 6).

1. What we do and don't collect

  • No sign-up — no name, email, or phone number is required, and there is no account to create or delete.
  • Your worship records, notes, bookmarks, reading positions and settings are never sent to any server.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no device fingerprinting, no tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency.
  • We do not build individual user profiles and we sell data to no one.
  • The app uses Google Firebase for anonymous usage statistics and announcements (section 8a).
  • The optional “Nearby mosques” page uses your location while it is open, and with internet sends it through our server to Google Places (section 6a).

3. Location

  • Location is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, for the optional weather display, and on the optional “Nearby mosques” page.
  • Prayer times and Qibla are calculated entirely on your device — for these, your location is not sent anywhere.
  • If the optional weather display is on, a coarse coordinate (rounded to roughly 11 km) is sent to the weather sources (section 7).
  • On the “Nearby mosques” page, while it is open and you are online, your coordinates are sent through our server to Google Places to find mosques around you (section 6a). This is the only case in which precise coordinates leave your device.
  • For statistics, the district/area you selected in the app is sent to Firebase, and Google derives a coarse country/city-level location from the IP address (section 8a). GPS coordinates are never sent to Firebase.
  • Location is used only while you are using the app. There is no background location access.
  • The app works without location permission; it then uses the place you select.
নামাজের সময় ও কিবলার হিসাব সম্পূর্ণ ফোনেই — এর জন্য অবস্থান কোথাও যায় না। আবহাওয়া চালু থাকলে মোটা দাগের স্থানাঙ্ক (~১১ কিমি) আবহাওয়ার উৎসে যায় (৭); পরিসংখ্যানের জন্য বেছে নেওয়া জেলা Firebase-এ যায় (৮ক)। "কাছের মসজিদ" পাতা খোলা থাকলে ও ইন্টারনেট থাকলে আশেপাশের মসজিদ খুঁজতে অবস্থান আমাদের সার্ভার হয়ে Google Places-এ যায় (৬ক) — নিখুঁত স্থানাঙ্ক ফোনের বাইরে যায় কেবল এখানেই। পেছনে (ব্যাকগ্রাউন্ডে) অবস্থান কখনো নেওয়া হয় না।

4. Data stored on your device

Everything below is written to the app's own sandboxed storage on your iPhone or iPad. The app does not send any of it to a server, and we cannot see any of it.

  • Worship records: which prayers you ticked, missed-prayer (qaza) counts, fasts, taraweeh nights and daily deeds.
  • Reading state: bookmarks and last-read verse.
  • Your own writing: personal notes — duas, intentions and goals you type in.
  • Counts and settings: tasbih totals, theme, chosen translation and reciter, calculation method, reminder preferences.
  • Downloads: Quran translations, recitation audio, Hafezi Quran pages and adhan tones you download.
  • Prayer-time location: the place you picked; if you use “current location”, also the last location fix, so times stay correct the next time you open the app. It stays on the device only.
  • Some of this reflects your religious practice. It is exactly the kind of information we made sure the app never sends anywhere — it is not included in statistics and is sent to no server. (It leaves the device only if you yourself create and share a backup file — section 5.)
  • iCloud / computer backups: if iCloud Backup (or a Finder/iTunes backup) is turned on for your device, iOS itself may include the app's data in that backup of your own device, so it can be restored on a new iPhone. That copy belongs to your Apple Account; we cannot see it.
বুকমার্ক, ট্র্যাকার, নোট, সেটিংস ও নামাজের সময়ের জায়গা — সব শুধু আপনার iPhone/iPad-এ। ফোনে iCloud Backup চালু থাকলে iOS নিজেই এগুলো আপনার নিজের iCloud ব্যাকআপে রাখতে পারে — আমরা দেখতে পাই না।

5. Sharing, copying and backup — actions you start

Nothing here happens on its own. Each of these runs only when you tap the button, and the data goes only where you send it — never to us.

  • Share: you can share a verse, hadith, quotation, dua or name of Allah to another app through the iOS share sheet. Only the text you chose is passed on.
  • Copy: tapping “copy” puts that text on your clipboard, as any app does. The app does not read your clipboard.
  • Backup: creates a single .json file containing your bookmarks, prayer tracker, qaza counts, fasts, taraweeh, daily deeds, reading positions, reminder preferences, app settings — and your personal notes, so that everything comes back on a new device. It is handed to the iOS share sheet — you decide whether it goes to Files, iCloud Drive, a chat or elsewhere. We never receive it.
    Note: because the backup file contains the duas, intentions and goals you wrote in your personal notes, think before sharing it with anyone.
  • Restore: reads a backup file you pick. If the file is not recognised, nothing is changed at all.
  • External links: the Facebook group link, the App Store page and directions in Google Maps open outside the app. From that point their own policies apply.
শেয়ার, কপি ও ব্যাকআপ — সবই আপনি চাপ দিলে তবেই হয়, আর যেখানে আপনি পাঠান সেখানেই যায়; আমাদের কাছে কখনো আসে না। ব্যাকআপ ফাইলে আপনার ব্যক্তিগত নোটও থাকে, তাই কারও সাথে শেয়ার করার আগে ভেবে নিন।

6a. Nearby mosques (optional)

  • On the device: to show how far away nearby mosques are and in which direction, the app uses your location, only while the page is open. Distances and directions are calculated on your device. The built-in mosque list comes from OpenStreetMap (and Overture Maps).
  • Nearby search (with internet): to show all mosques around you, the app sends your latitude/longitude to our server namaj-config.duetrackbd.workers.dev (hosted on Cloudflare), together with a random anonymous ID that the app generates itself — it is not a hardware ID and not the advertising identifier. Our server forwards only the location to the Google Places API, which returns the nearby mosques.
  • What our server keeps: neither your location nor Google's results are stored on our server. The anonymous ID is hashed immediately and used only to count how many searches a device made that day, so the free daily quota stays available for everyone; that count expires after about 2 days.
  • Area list: to fetch the up-to-date OpenStreetMap list for your area, only the number of a grid cell of about 28 km is sent — not your coordinates.
  • “Add a mosque here”: only if you tap it yourself, the mosque name you type and your current location (as the mosque's location) are sent to our server. The proposal is stored with a short hash of the anonymous ID and a short hash of your network address (never the raw ID or IP address) — used only to count proposals per day and to prevent abuse. After review, the mosque (its name and location only) may appear in everyone's list. Your name or any other information about you is not sent.
ইন্টারনেট থাকলে আশেপাশের মসজিদ দেখাতে আপনার অবস্থান আমাদের সার্ভার হয়ে Google Places-এ যায়, সাথে অ্যাপের বানানো একটি এলোমেলো নামহীন আইডি (ফোনের আসল আইডি নয়)। আমাদের সার্ভারে অবস্থান বা Google-এর ফল জমানো হয় না; আইডিটি সাথে সাথে হ্যাশ করা হয় ও দুই দিনে মুছে যায়। "মসজিদ যোগ করুন" চাপলে কেবল মসজিদের নাম ও তখনকার অবস্থান যায়; যাচাইয়ের পর মসজিদটি সবার তালিকায় যেতে পারে।

7. Weather (optional)

  • The home-screen sky shows temperature and clouds using two open sources — MET Norway and Open-Meteo.
  • Only a latitude/longitude rounded to one decimal (a cell of roughly 11 km) is sent. No name, ID or device identifier accompanies it.
  • Requested at most once every 15 minutes while the app is open; the last result is cached on the device.
  • Weather can be turned off in Settings → Weather (inside the app), after which no request is made.

8. Notifications

  • Prayer-time reminders and adhan alerts are scheduled on your device; no internet is required for them.
  • We may occasionally send an announcement through Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple's push service — for example a Ramadan or Eid date, a government holiday or a correction.
  • Notifications are never used for advertising, promotion or marketing, and never carry personal information.
  • Reminders can be turned off inside the app, all together or per prayer. All notifications from the app, including announcements, can be turned off in iOS Settings → Notifications → নামাজ শিক্ষা.

8a. Usage statistics and announcements (Google Firebase)

The app includes Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Google processes this data as our service provider.

  • What is collected: app-lifecycle events such as app opens, screens viewed and session length; notification received/opened events; app version, iOS version and device model; a random per-installation identifier (the Firebase app-instance ID); Apple's identifier for vendor (IDFV), which Firebase uses on iOS; a coarse location Google derives from the IP address; and the district/area you selected in the app.
  • Not collected: the advertising identifier (IDFA). The iOS app is built without advertising-ID support, does not link Apple's AdSupport framework and does not ask for tracking permission. Ad-personalisation signals are switched off.
  • Notification token: Cloud Messaging creates a token linked to Apple's push token so announcements can reach the device. All installations are subscribed to one shared topic, plus a topic for the country of the location you picked (for example “Bangladesh”, or an “expatriate” topic when abroad) — country only, never city or location. We never keep a list of tokens.
  • Why: to know how many people use the app, which features and districts need the most attention, and to send important announcements.
  • What we see: aggregated numbers only (for example active users or how many devices opened an announcement). We cannot see your name, and nothing tells us what you read or how you pray.
  • Never sent to Firebase: worship records, notes, bookmarks, Quran reading positions, GPS coordinates, contacts or the advertising identifier.
  • Retention: Google deletes event-level data after the retention period set in Google Analytics (at most 14 months).
Firebase Analytics ও Cloud Messaging: অ্যাপ খোলা, কোন পর্দা দেখা হলো, নোটিফিকেশন পৌঁছানো/খোলা, অ্যাপ ও iOS-এর সংস্করণ, ফোনের মডেল, একটি এলোমেলো ইনস্টলেশন-আইডি, Apple-এর IDFV, আইপি থেকে আনুমানিক অবস্থান, আর বেছে নেওয়া জেলা। বিজ্ঞাপন-আইডি (IDFA) কখনো নেওয়া হয় না। আমরা দেখি কেবল সামগ্রিক সংখ্যা।

10. Data retention, withdrawing consent and deletion

  • We keep no database of users, and none of your writing, records or reading data is stored on any server. Our settings server stores app settings and the names and locations of mosques that users have proposed (section 6a), each with short hashes for abuse prevention — never anyone's name or identity. Approved mosques (name and location only) are shown to all users; rejected proposals are deleted.
  • For the nearby-mosque search, your location and Google's results are not stored on our server; the hashed per-device daily search count expires after about 2 days. Cloudflare may keep short-lived technical request logs, as any web host does.
  • Usage statistics are retained by Google Firebase as described in section 8a.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can stop each kind of data from leaving your device at any time — location: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → নামাজ শিক্ষা → Never (or turn Precise Location off); notifications and announcements: iOS Settings → Notifications → নামাজ শিক্ষা; weather: in the app, Settings → Weather; nearby search: simply don't open the Nearby mosques page.
  • Deleting data on your device: delete the app (touch and hold the icon → Remove App → Delete App). This permanently removes everything the app saved, and also resets the Firebase app-instance ID. (Note: iOS “Offload App” keeps your data; choose Delete App to remove it.) Downloaded files can also be removed individually inside the app (More → Downloads · আরও → ডাউনলোড).
  • Asking us to delete data: if you proposed a mosque and want it removed, or have any other deletion request, email support@duetrack.bd. Because we hold nothing linked to your identity, please describe the data (for example the mosque name and area); we will delete anything we can find and reply within 7 days.
  • There is no account to delete.
