3. Location

  • Location is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, for the optional weather display, and on the optional “Nearby mosques” page.
  • For prayer times and Qibla only an approximate location is requested, and the calculation happens entirely on your device — for these, your location is not sent anywhere.
  • If the optional weather display is on, a coarse coordinate (rounded to roughly 11 km) is sent to the weather sources (section 7).
  • Precise location is requested only on the “Nearby mosques” page. While that page is open and you are online, your precise coordinates are sent through our server to Google Places to find mosques around you (section 6a). This is the only case in which precise GPS coordinates leave your phone.
  • For statistics, the district/area you selected in the app is sent to Firebase, and Google derives a coarse country/city-level location from the IP address (section 8a). GPS coordinates are never sent to Firebase.
  • We request location only while you are using the app. There is no background location access.
  • The app works without location permission; it then uses the district you select.

4. Data stored on your device

Everything below is written to your phone's own app-private storage. The app does not send any of it to a server, and we cannot see any of it.

  • Worship records: which prayers you ticked, missed-prayer (qaza) counts, fasts, taraweeh nights and daily deeds.
  • Reading state: bookmarks and last-read verse.
  • Your own writing: personal notes — duas, intentions and goals you type in.
  • Counts and settings: tasbih totals, theme, chosen translation and reciter, calculation method, reminder preferences.
  • Downloads: Quran translations (with footnotes, where the translation has them) and recitation audio.
  • Prayer-time location: the district or city you picked; if you use “current location”, also the last location fix, so times stay correct the next time you open the app. It stays on the phone only.
  • Home-screen widget: if you add it, the next 30 days of prayer times are saved for the widget so it works without opening the app.
  • Some of this reflects your religious practice. It is exactly the kind of information we made sure the app never sends anywhere — it is not included in statistics and is sent to no server. (It leaves the phone only if you yourself create and share a backup file — section 5.)
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of it.
  • If Android's own Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android itself may back up the app's settings and records to your own Google account so you can restore them on a new phone. That copy lives in your account; we cannot see it.

5. Sharing, copying and backup — actions you start

Nothing here happens on its own. Each of these runs only when you tap the button, and the data goes only where you send it — never to us.

  • Share: you can share a verse, hadith, quotation, dua or name of Allah to another app (WhatsApp, Messenger, email…). Android's own share sheet decides where it goes. Only the text you chose is passed on.
  • Copy: tapping “copy” puts that text on your clipboard, as any app does.
  • Backup: creates a single .json file containing your bookmarks, prayer tracker, qaza counts, fasts, taraweeh, daily deeds (amol), reading positions, reminder preferences, app settings — and your personal notes, so that everything comes back on a new phone. It is handed to Android's share sheet — you decide whether it goes to Drive, a chat, or your own storage. We never receive it.
    Note: because the backup file contains the duas, intentions and goals you wrote in your personal notes, think before sharing it with anyone.
  • Restore: reads a backup file you pick. If the file is not recognised, nothing is changed at all — a half-restore that damages your data is not allowed.
  • External links: the Facebook group link and the Play Store rating page open outside the app, in Facebook or Play. From that point their own policies apply.

6a. Nearby mosques (optional)

  • On the device: to show how far away nearby mosques are and in which direction, the app uses your precise location — requested only on this page, and used only while the page is open. Distances and directions are calculated on your phone. The built-in mosque list comes from OpenStreetMap (and Overture Maps).
  • Nearby search (with internet): to show all mosques around you, the app sends your latitude/longitude (6 decimal places) to our server namaj-config.duetrackbd.workers.dev (hosted on Cloudflare), together with a random anonymous ID that the app generates itself — it is not a hardware ID and not the advertising ID. Our server forwards only the location to the Google Places API, which returns the nearby mosques.
  • What our server keeps: neither your location nor Google's results are stored on our server. The anonymous ID is hashed immediately and used only to count how many searches a phone made that day, so the free daily quota stays available for everyone; that count expires after about 2 days. Google's processing of the location it receives is governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Area list: to fetch the up-to-date OpenStreetMap list for your area, only the number of a grid cell of about 28 km is sent — not your coordinates.
  • “Add a mosque here”: only if you tap it yourself, the mosque name you type and your current location (as the mosque's location) are sent to our server. The proposal is stored together with a short hash of the anonymous ID and a short hash of your network address (never the raw ID or IP address) — used only to count how many proposals arrive per day and to prevent abuse. After review, the mosque (its name and location only) may appear in everyone's list. Your name or any other information about you is not sent.
  • Directions: tapping a mosque for directions opens Google Maps outside the app; from there Google's own policy applies.

8a. Usage statistics and announcements (Google Firebase)

From version 1.0.1 the app includes Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Google processes this data as our service provider.

  • What is collected: app-lifecycle events such as app opens, screens viewed and session length; notification received/opened events; app version, Android version and device model; a random per-installation identifier (the Firebase installation / app-instance ID); a coarse location Google derives from the IP address; and the district/area you selected in the app.
  • Notification token: Cloud Messaging creates a token so announcements can reach the phone. All installations are subscribed to one shared topic, plus a topic for the country of the location you picked (for example “Bangladesh” or “Saudi Arabia”, and an “expatriate” topic when abroad) so that country-specific announcements such as Ramadan or Eid dates reach the right phones — country only, never city or location. We never keep a list of tokens.
  • Why: to know how many people use the app, which features and districts need the most attention, and to send important announcements.
  • What we see: aggregated numbers only (for example active users or how many phones opened an announcement). We cannot see your name, and nothing tells us what you read or how you pray.
  • Never sent to Firebase: worship records, notes, bookmarks, Quran reading positions, GPS coordinates, contacts, or the advertising ID. Advertising-ID collection and ad-personalisation signals are switched off in the app.
  • Retention: Google deletes event-level data after the retention period set in Google Analytics (at most 14 months).
সংস্করণ ১.০.১ থেকে Firebase Analytics ও Cloud Messaging: অ্যাপ খোলা, কোন পর্দা দেখা হলো, নোটিফিকেশন পৌঁছানো/খোলা, অ্যাপ ও ফোনের সংস্করণ, একটি এলোমেলো ইনস্টলেশন-আইডি, আইপি থেকে আনুমানিক অবস্থান, আর আপনার বেছে নেওয়া জেলা। আমরা দেখি কেবল সামগ্রিক সংখ্যা। ইবাদতের হিসাব, নোট, GPS বা বিজ্ঞাপন-আইডি Firebase-এ কখনো যায় না।

9. Google Play services

  • In-app update: the app asks Google Play whether a newer version exists, so you can update without leaving the app.
  • In-app review: after some use, Google Play's own rating sheet may appear once. No question is asked before it and no one is filtered or selected. We are never told who rated or what they rated.
  • Read-aloud: reading the Bengali meaning of the Quran aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine (for example Google Speech Services); the app itself sends nothing for this.
  • These use Google's official libraries or your phone's system services. Any data handling there is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • We keep no database of users, and none of your writing, records or reading data is stored on any server. Our settings server stores app settings (such as the minimum supported version) and the names and locations of mosques that users have proposed (section 6a), each with a short hash of the anonymous ID and of the network address for abuse prevention — never anyone's name or identity. Approved mosques (name and location only) are shown to all users; rejected proposals are deleted.
  • For the nearby-mosque search, your location and Google's results are not stored on our server; the hashed per-device daily search count expires after about 2 days. Cloudflare may keep short-lived technical request logs, as any web host does.
  • Usage statistics are retained by Google Firebase as described in section 8a. The weather services process the approximate coordinate they receive under their own terms, and Google processes the location sent to Google Places under Google's privacy policy.
  • Everything the app saves stays on your device for as long as the app is installed.
  • To delete it: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → “নামাজ শিক্ষা” (Namaz Shikkha) → Storage → Clear data. Either removes all app data permanently.
  • Downloaded translation and audio files can also be removed individually inside the app (More → Downloads · আরও → ডাউনলোড).
  • There is no account to delete and no request you need to send us — the delete button is on your own phone.
আমাদের কোনো ব্যবহারকারী-ডেটাবেজ নেই; আপনার লেখা, হিসাব বা পড়ার তথ্য কোনো সার্ভারে জমা হয় না। আমাদের সেটিংস-সার্ভারে থাকে কেবল অ্যাপের সেটিংস আর ব্যবহারকারীদের প্রস্তাব করা মসজিদের নাম ও অবস্থান — কারও নাম-পরিচয় নয়। মসজিদ খোঁজার অবস্থান জমানো হয় না; দিনের গোনা দুই দিনে মুছে যায়। পরিসংখ্যান Firebase-এ নির্ধারিত মেয়াদ পর্যন্ত থাকে। অ্যাপ আনইনস্টল করলে বা "Clear data" করলে সব মুছে যায়; ডাউনলোড করা ফাইল অ্যাপের ভেতর থেকেও আলাদা করে মোছা যায়।

11. Security

  • All network requests use HTTPS (encrypted in transit).
  • App data is kept in Android's app-private storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • We hold no user database of our own. The only user-contributed data on our server is proposed mosque names and locations (with short hashes, no identity), so there is no store of personal data on our side that could be breached.
সব সংযোগ HTTPS; অ্যাপের তথ্য ফোনের সুরক্ষিত ব্যক্তিগত ফোল্ডারে থাকে। আমাদের নিজেদের কোনো ব্যবহারকারী-ডেটাবেজ নেই — সার্ভারে আছে কেবল প্রস্তাবিত মসজিদের নাম ও অবস্থান।

12. No sharing, no selling

Both are used only to make that feature work.
