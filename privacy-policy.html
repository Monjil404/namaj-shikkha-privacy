This app is fully offline-first and ad-free. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data. There is no account, no tracking, and no advertising SDK. We run no server of our own, so there is nowhere for your data to go. The sections below explain exactly what the app does.
এই অ্যাপটি সম্পূর্ণ অফলাইন ও বিজ্ঞাপনমুক্ত। আমরা আপনার কোনো ব্যক্তিগত তথ্য সংগ্রহ, সংরক্ষণ বা বিক্রি করি না। আমাদের নিজস্ব কোনো সার্ভারই নেই।

1. We do not collect personal data

  • No sign-up — no name, email, or phone number is required.
  • No data is sent to any server of ours, because the app has no server of its own.
  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDK, no device fingerprinting, no advertising ID.
  • We do not build user profiles and we sell data to no one.
অ্যাকাউন্ট লাগে না; কোনো তথ্য আমাদের সার্ভারে যায় না (সার্ভারই নেই); কোনো বিজ্ঞাপন, অ্যানালিটিক্স বা ট্র্যাকিং নেই।

2. Permissions the app asks for, and why

Every permission below is optional. If you decline one, only the feature that needs it stops working — the rest of the app continues normally.

PermissionWhy it is usedIf you decline
ACCESS_COARSE / FINE_LOCATION Prayer times and Qibla direction, calculated on your device. The app uses the district/upazila you pick from a built-in list.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Prayer-time reminders and adhan alerts. No reminders; times are still shown inside the app.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM So the adhan fires at the exact minute rather than being delayed by the system. The app falls back to inexact alarms, which may be a few minutes late.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Re-schedules your reminders after the phone restarts. —
FOREGROUND_SERVICE (mediaPlayback) Keeps Quran recitation playing when the screen is off, with lock-screen controls. —
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE One-time download of Quran text, translations, hadith books and recitation audio; optional weather. —
VIBRATE, WAKE_LOCK Vibration for reminders; keeping the device awake while an adhan plays. —
Compass & motion sensors (no permission needed) The Qibla screen reads the magnetometer and accelerometer to point towards the Kaaba, and applies a magnetic-declination correction. Readings are used in the moment and never stored or sent anywhere. The Qibla direction is still shown as an angle you can align by hand.
File picker (no permission) Only if you choose your own adhan/tone file. The app reads just that one file you picked. Built-in tones are used.
প্রতিটি অনুমতি ঐচ্ছিক। না দিলে কেবল ওই ফিচারটিই বন্ধ থাকে, বাকি অ্যাপ আগের মতোই চলে।

3. Location

  • Location is used only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction.
  • The calculation happens entirely on your device — your location is never uploaded to us, and we operate no server that could receive it.
  • One exception: if the optional weather display is on, a coarse coordinate is sent to the weather sources. See section 7.
  • We request location only while you are using the app. There is no background location access.
  • The app works without location permission; it then uses the district you select.
অবস্থান শুধু নামাজের সময় ও কিবলা বের করতে, হিসাব সম্পূর্ণ ফোনেই। একটিই ব্যতিক্রম — আবহাওয়া চালু থাকলে মোটা দাগের স্থানাঙ্ক আবহাওয়ার উৎসে যায় (৭ নং অংশ)।

4. Data stored on your device

Everything below is written to your phone's own app-private storage. None of it is transmitted anywhere, and we cannot see any of it.

  • Worship records: which prayers you ticked, missed-prayer (qaza) counts, fasts, taraweeh nights and daily deeds.
  • Reading state: bookmarks, last-read verse and hadith position.
  • Your own writing: personal notes — duas, intentions and goals you type in.
  • Counts and settings: tasbih totals, theme, chosen translation and reciter, calculation method, reminder preferences.
  • Downloads: Quran text, translations, hadith books and recitation audio.
  • Some of this reflects your religious practice. It is exactly the kind of information we made sure never leaves your device — there is no server for it to go to.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of it.
বুকমার্ক, ট্র্যাকার, সেটিংস — সব শুধু আপনার ফোনেই; অ্যাপ মুছলে মুছে যায়।

5. Sharing, copying and backup — actions you start

Nothing here happens on its own. Each of these runs only when you tap the button, and the data goes only where you send it — never to us.

  • Share: you can share a verse, hadith, quotation, dua or name of Allah to another app (WhatsApp, Messenger, email…). Android's own share sheet decides where it goes. Only the text you chose is passed on.
  • Copy: tapping “copy” puts that text on your clipboard, as any app does.
  • Backup: creates a single .json file containing your bookmarks, prayer tracker, qaza counts, fasts, taraweeh, deeds, reading positions, reminder preferences and app settings. It is handed to Android's share sheet — you decide whether it goes to Drive, a chat, or your own storage. We never receive it.
    Note: your personal notes are not included in the backup file.
  • Restore: reads a backup file you pick. If the file is not recognised, nothing is changed at all — a half-restore that damages your data is not allowed.
  • External links: the Facebook group link and the Play Store rating page open outside the app, in Facebook or Play. From that point their own policies apply.
শেয়ার, কপি ও ব্যাকআপ — সবই আপনি চাপ দিলে তবেই হয়, আর যেখানে আপনি পাঠান সেখানেই যায়; আমাদের কাছে কখনো আসে না। ব্যাকআপ ফাইলে ব্যক্তিগত নোট থাকে না।

7. Weather (optional)

  • The home-screen sky shows temperature and clouds using two open sources — MET Norway and Open-Meteo.
  • Only a latitude/longitude is sent, rounded to one decimal — a cell of roughly 11 km. If GPS is on, that coordinate is derived from your own position, but it is far too coarse to identify you. No name, ID or device identifier accompanies it.
  • Requested at most once every 30 minutes; the last result is cached on the device.
  • It can be turned off entirely in Settings → Weather, after which no request is made.
আবহাওয়া MET Norway ও Open-Meteo থেকে; কেবল স্থানাঙ্ক যায়, এক দশমিকে গোল করা (প্রায় ১১ কিমির ঘর); নাম বা পরিচয় কিছুই যায় না; সেটিংস → আবহাওয়া বন্ধ করা যায়।

8. Notifications

  • Notifications are used for prayer-time reminders only — never for marketing.
  • The times are computed on your device; no internet is required.
  • They can be turned off anytime in Settings, or per prayer.
নামাজের রিমাইন্ডার নোটিফিকেশন; সময় ফোনেই হিসাব হয়; সেটিংস থেকে বন্ধ করা যায়।

9. Google Play services

  • In-app update: the app asks Google Play whether a newer version exists, so you can update without leaving the app.
  • In-app review: after a few minutes of use we ask once whether you like the app. If you say yes, Google Play's own rating sheet opens. We are never told who rated or what they rated.
  • These use Google's official libraries. Any data handling there is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours.
অ্যাপ আপডেট ও রেটিং — গুগলের নিজস্ব ব্যবস্থা; কে রেটিং দিলেন তা আমরা জানতে পারি না।

10. Data retention and deletion

  • We retain nothing, because we collect nothing and operate no server or database.
  • Everything the app saves stays on your device for as long as the app is installed.
  • To delete it: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → “নামাজ শিক্ষা” (Namaz Shikkha) → Storage → Clear data. Either removes all app data permanently.
  • Downloaded Quran/hadith/audio files can also be removed individually inside the app (More → Downloads · আরও → ডাউনলোড).
  • There is no account to delete and no request you need to send us — the delete button is on your own phone.
আমরা কিছুই রাখি না। অ্যাপ আনইনস্টল করলে বা "Clear data" করলে সব মুছে যায়; ডাউনলোড করা ফাইল অ্যাপের ভেতর থেকেও আলাদা করে মোছা যায়।

11. Security

  • All network requests use HTTPS (encrypted in transit).
  • App data is kept in Android's app-private storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • We hold no user database, so there is no store of personal data that could be breached.
সব সংযোগ HTTPS; অ্যাপের তথ্য ফোনের সুরক্ষিত ব্যক্তিগত ফোল্ডারে থাকে।

12. No sharing, no selling

  • No advertisers, no data brokers, no analytics firms receive anything. The app contains no such SDK.
  • The only user data that leaves the device is the coarse weather coordinate described in section 7, sent to MET Norway and Open-Meteo so they can return a forecast. Google Play declares this as Approximate location — shared, for app functionality.
  • We transfer no data for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • We would only disclose information if legally compelled — and we hold none to disclose.
বিজ্ঞাপনদাতা, ডেটা ব্রোকার বা অ্যানালিটিক্স কোম্পানি — কারও কাছে কিছুই যায় না, বিক্রি তো নয়ই। ফোনের বাইরে যায় কেবল ৭ নং অংশে বলা মোটা দাগের আবহাওয়া-স্থানাঙ্ক।

15. What this app never does

  • It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, call log, SMS, photos or files — the only exception is the single audio file you pick yourself as a custom adhan.
  • It does not scan the apps installed on your phone. Android requires apps to declare which other apps they may open; we declare exactly three — Facebook, Facebook Lite and Google Play — and nothing else is looked at.
  • It sends us no crash reports, no diagnostics and no usage statistics. There is no analytics of any kind. (Google Play may collect its own crash data for any Android app; that is Google's, governed by their policy, and we cannot identify anyone from it.)
  • It shows no advertising, contains no ad SDK and no advertising identifier.
  • It has no account, no login and no in-app purchase.
