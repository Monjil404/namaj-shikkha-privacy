Namaz Shikkha, Quran & Hadith

নামাজ শিক্ষা, কুরআন ও হাদিস

A free, ad-free, offline-first Islamic app in Bengali — prayer times for Bangladesh and for Bengali speakers abroad, Qibla, the Quran with Bengali translation and recitation, duas and step-by-step prayer learning. No account and no ads. Your worship records and notes stay on your phone. Anonymous usage statistics are collected, and location is used only for app features such as prayer times, weather and finding nearby mosques (see the Privacy Policy).

This site hosts the app's official policies — separately for Android and for iPhone & iPad.
