3. Hadith

  • The app offers no hadith book downloads. Earlier versions offered Bengali translations of Bukhari, Muslim and other collections from a public dataset. That dataset's Bengali source publishes no licence, and the translations are the work of Bangladeshi publishers — so we removed them entirely (September 2026). Copies left on a phone by an earlier version are deleted automatically.
  • Hadith quoted elsewhere in the app — in duas, daily deeds and the daily quote — are shown with their book name and hadith number, and the grading where one is stated.
  • The 439 quotations in the app’s daily-quote feature (“আজকের বাণী”) were each checked one by one against the reference collections before release.
  • If you are a translator or publisher and believe any wording in the app is yours, please contact us. We will credit you as you wish, or remove it — whichever you prefer.
