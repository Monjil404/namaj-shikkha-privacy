6. About prayer times

  • Prayer times are derived by astronomical calculation, based on your location, the calculation method you choose, and precaution (ihtiyat) minutes.
  • Times can differ by a few minutes between localities and between methods. If in doubt, give precedence to your local mosque or the official timetable of your country (in Bangladesh, the Islamic Foundation Bangladesh timetable).
  • For fasting, Eid and moon sighting, the final decision rests with the moon-sighting authority of your country (in Bangladesh, the National Moon Sighting Committee).
